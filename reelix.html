Reelix.

Free streaming where every pick explains itself and no ad ambushes you.

RoleProduct strategy, UX, prototyping
PlatformMobile, web, connected TV
TypeProduct concept
OutputWorking prototype

The problem

Free streaming apps show a wall of posters, then an ad that cuts in mid-scene.

What I designed

Picks that say why they fit, ads you can see coming, and paying only when you choose to.

The value

Viewers stay through the free tier instead of leaving it. Trust becomes the retention lever.

Product concept. Not affiliated with or endorsed by any streaming service. Titles, prices and brands are fictional; photos from Unsplash.

02 · Key insight

Viewers don't mind free. They mind feeling tricked.

Ads and paywalls are tolerated when they're predictable and optional. So every decision — what to watch, when an ad lands, when to pay — sits on the viewer's side of the table.

03 · Key decisions

Three decisions that shaped the product.

01

Every pick explains itself

Discovery starts from mood, time and language. Each pick says why it fits — "matches your funny mood". Nothing fits? One tap loosens a filter instead of a dead end.

Trade-off: less room on each card, for more trust in what's on it.
02

No ambush ads

Breaks are marked on the timeline with a 5-second warning. Skip after 5s, "Why this ad?" always there. Playback resumes 2s before the break, and the next episode skips the pre-roll.

Trade-off: fewer ad slots, for viewers who stay through them.
03

Paying is opt-in

Vertical micro-dramas are free for 5 episodes. At episode 6: watch a 15-second ad, go Premium, or rewatch. New films can be rented, with terms shown before paying.

Trade-off: a softer paywall, for a free path that never dead-ends.
04 · The experience

One product, from a phone to the living room.

Ask, then check

A sentence becomes filter chips you can see and remove. If nothing fits, it says what it loosened.

Rent with clear terms

30 days to start, 48 hours to finish — before you pay.

TV mode

Large focus tiles, arrow keys and Enter only. Nothing to type.

Mobile + Hindi

Same system at 390px, with a Hindi interface.

05 · Impact

What I'd expect it to change.

These are hypotheses, not results. Each is wired into the prototype as an A/B test, with a guardrail that must not get worse.

What I took away

Show the working.

A reason turns a recommendation from an ad for the catalogue into advice.

Predictable beats fewer.

An ad you saw coming costs less than one that surprises you.

Always leave a free path.

Paying feels like a choice only when not paying is one too.

Every button in the prototype works. Go break it.
